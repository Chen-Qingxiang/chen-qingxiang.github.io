About

Research is how I ask.
Tools are how I think.

I’m Qingxiang Chen, a researcher based in Sydney. My professional work centres on X-ray imaging and CT; outside the lab, I build small, durable tools for historical research and self-directed learning.

These projects share one idea: understanding grows when information becomes spatial, interactive, and traceable—something you can zoom, compare, question, and return to.

  • 01

    Scientific imaging

    X-ray systems, CT, detectors, and quantitative experiments.

  • 02

    Digital humanities

    Timelines, maps, institutions, biographies, and source-aware data.

  • 03

    Learning tools

    Interactive visual models that make difficult ideas easier to feel.
