Combine rule: The output keeps the page size of the front scan. Its upper half comes from the front PDF and its lower half from the back PDF. With scans from the same scanner, the ID size and position are largely preserved.

Privacy and implementation

This tool has no file-upload endpoint, backend service, account system, or analytics. PDFs are read by your browser and processed in memory with a pinned version of pdf-lib. Generated files are downloaded directly by the browser.

Note: the page loads pdf-lib 1.17.1 from jsDelivr when opened, so an internet connection is needed for the library. Your selected PDF files are not sent to jsDelivr or this site's server.
