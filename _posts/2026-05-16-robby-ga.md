> **Project:** [Robby GA](https://chen-qingxiang.github.io/robby-ga/)

Robby the Robot is a grid-world agent. The world contains empty cells and cans. Robby sees only a small local neighborhood, chooses an action, and receives rewards or penalties.

The interesting part is the policy representation. Robby observes five locations:

1. current cell
2. north
3. south
4. west
5. east

Each location can be empty, can, or wall. That gives:

```text
3^5 = 243
```

possible perceptual states. A policy is an array of 243 actions.

## What the page does

The project has two connected parts:

1. a Robby grid-world demo
2. a genetic algorithm trainer

In the demo, you can run a random policy, a simple hand-coded baseline, or the best evolved policy after training.

In the trainer, the GA evolves policies by testing them across many random worlds and selecting policies with higher average scores.

## Reward scheme

The rewards are simple:

- successful pickup: `+10`
- pickup on an empty cell: `-1`
- moving into a wall: `-5`
- valid movement: `0`
- stay: `0`

A policy has to learn useful local behavior, such as picking up a can when standing on one, moving toward nearby cans, and avoiding wall collisions.

## Why I made it

Robby is a useful step beyond simple bit-string optimization. In OneMax, fitness can be read directly from the genome. For Robby, fitness comes from behavior in an environment.

The same policy can score differently in different random worlds. Averaging over many trials makes the evaluation more stable. This connects the GA to a simple agent-environment loop.

## What to look for

Start with the hand-coded policy. It should behave sensibly, but it is not carefully optimized.

Then train a population and run the best evolved policy in the demo. The policy inspection panel is also useful: it shows how many entries choose each action and how the policy responds to selected percepts.

A single entry in the 243-action table is hard to judge by itself. The policy matters through the behavior produced over many steps.
